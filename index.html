How revexos.com is organized

revexos.com is built to be a reference on quote-to-cash (Q2C): the path from a quote to cash in the bank. Each section of the site does one job. This page explains each section, what it is meant to do, and how the sections link together.

Written for contributors, partners, and anyone trying to find their way around the site. Live site: revexos.com

Blog

The blog explains quote-to-cash in depth. Each post answers one real question a CFO, controller, founder or RevOps lead would type into Google or ask an AI assistant. Posts are specific and practical, not sales pitches. revexos.com/blog

Four categories (post format)

  • best-practices Explainers, "X vs Y" comparisons, "Best tools for..." roundups and decision guides. Helps readers understand a topic and choose.
  • tutorials Step-by-step builds: automations in n8n, dashboards with Claude Cowork, export guides for QuickBooks and Xero. Helps readers do it themselves.
  • product-updates Launches and changes to RevExOS tools.
  • company-news Why RevExOS exists and how we think about the Q2C problem.

Topic clusters (post subject)

  • AR and collections DSO, dunning, collections emails, AR by industry
  • Invoicing generators, invoice parsing and OCR, PO matching, exports
  • Quotes and proposals templates, quote vs estimate, scope control
  • Revenue recognition ASC 606 for services, SaaS and AI pricing
  • Usage and AI billing metering, token billing, Stripe/Metronome/Orb
  • Billing platforms SAP BRIM, Zuora, Salesforce Revenue Cloud, Merchant of Record
  • AI in Q2C Claude workflows, prompts and playbooks for finance teams

What every post includes

  1. A question-shaped titleMatches how people search and how they ask AI assistants.
  2. The answer firstA direct answer at the top, followed by the detail, examples and edge cases.
  3. Specifics over generalitiesReal numbers, named tools, actual workflows and prompts.
  4. FAQ sectionShort, citable answers marked up as FAQ schema so they can surface in search results and AI answers.
  5. Links onwardTo the matching platform page, the free tool that does the task, and a related case study.
  6. A unique cover imageEvery post gets its own image. Covers are never shared between posts.

Case studies

Case studies back up the blog. Each one describes an automation we actually built: what the process looked like before and after, the tools used, and the results. revexos.com/case-studies

Two groups

Order-to-cash 7 studies on the core Q2C work: invoice and PO parsing, invoice generation, payment reconciliation, dispute management, end-to-end AR, order fulfilment, and AI knowledge bases built on financial data.

Broader automation 13 studies covering related automation work: cold email and calling, lead generation, content pipelines, AI agents, CRM and data sync, reporting, and infrastructure. They show the range of the team.

A toggle on the case studies page switches between the two groups.

What every case study includes

  1. BeforeThe manual process and what it cost.
  2. AfterWhat runs automatically now.
  3. StackThe tools used. This is how a case study shows up on platform pages.
  4. How it worksThe workflow, step by step.
  5. ResultsMeasured outcomes, such as hours saved or faster payment.
  6. Related linksThe blog posts and tools for the same problem.

Editorial principles

  • Teach first, sell second. Content that genuinely helps gets cited. Sales pitches do not.
  • Deep and specific. Named tools, real workflows, real numbers. No generic overviews.
  • Written for search engines and AI assistants. Clear questions, direct answers, structured FAQs.
  • Plain, direct prose. No filler.
  • Everything is connected. Posts, platforms, tools and case studies link to each other around the Q2C stages.
