How revexos.com is organized

revexos.com is a learning hub for lead-to-cash and quote-to-cash (Q2C): the path from a lead to cash in the bank and revenue on the books. It teaches how to turn messy quoting, invoicing and collections processes into working systems with automation and AI, and shows the projects behind that knowledge. Each section of the site does one job. This page explains each one and how they link together.

Written for contributors, partners, and anyone trying to find their way around the site. Live site: revexos.com · Updated October 2026.

Blog

The blog explains lead-to-cash in depth. Each post answers one real question a CFO, controller, founder or RevOps lead would type into Google or ask an AI assistant. Posts are specific and practical, not sales pitches. revexos.com/blog

Categories (post format)

  • best-practices Explainers, "X vs Y" comparisons, "Best tools for..." roundups and decision guides. Helps readers understand a topic and choose.
  • tutorials Step-by-step builds: automations in n8n, dashboards with Claude Cowork, export guides for QuickBooks and Xero. Helps readers do it themselves.
  • product-updates Launches and changes to RevExOS tools.
  • company-news Why RevExOS exists and how it approaches the Q2C problem.

Topic clusters (post subject)

  • AR and collections DSO, dunning, collections emails, cash forecasting
  • Invoicing generators, invoice parsing and OCR, PO matching, exports
  • Quotes and proposals templates, quote vs estimate, scope control
  • Revenue recognition ASC 606 for services, SaaS and AI pricing
  • Usage and AI billing metering, token billing, Stripe/Metronome/Orb
  • Billing platforms SAP BRIM, Zuora, Salesforce Revenue Cloud, Merchant of Record
  • AI in Q2C Claude workflows, prompts and playbooks for finance teams

What every post includes

  1. A question-shaped titleMatches how people search and how they ask AI assistants.
  2. The answer firstA direct answer at the top, followed by the detail, examples and edge cases.
  3. Specifics over generalitiesReal numbers, named tools, actual workflows and prompts.
  4. FAQ sectionShort, citable answers marked up as FAQ schema so they can surface in search results and AI answers.
  5. A lead-to-cash stageEvery post is mapped to one stage, which places it on that stage's hub page and in llms.txt.
  6. Links onwardTo the matching platform page, the free tool that does the task, and related posts.
  7. A unique cover imageEvery post gets its own image. Covers are never shared between posts.

Editorial principles

  • Teach, don't sell. Content pages end with the next thing to learn or try, not a sales pitch. Content that genuinely helps gets cited.
  • Deep and specific. Named tools, real workflows, real numbers, worked examples. No generic overviews.
  • Written for search engines and AI assistants. Clear questions, direct answers, structured FAQs, and an llms.txt index.
  • Plain, direct prose. No filler.
  • Everything is connected. Posts, platforms, tools and projects link to each other around the lead-to-cash stages.
